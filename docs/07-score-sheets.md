# Chapter 7 — Score-Sheet Templates

Two printable templates follow. Make one copy per candidate, and one per
panelist — **every panelist scores independently before any discussion**
(Chapter 5 explains why). Fill the anchored-scale row in *before* the first
interview of the day, not after.

**Notes that stay off the sheet** (privacy and fairness): family status,

a room with the candidate's lawyer standing comfortably in the doorway.

## Calibrating between panelists

- If two panelists differ by more than one point on the same answer,
  **discuss the behavior, not the person**: "What did you hear that I didn't?"
- Keep one sheet per *question*, not one per candidate summary. The mismatch
  is almost always within a single answer, and the fix is an anchor rewrite,
  not a panelist re-education.
- Once per hiring round, cut the weakest question and rewrite its anchored
  scale. Sheets that never change stop measuring anything after a while.
